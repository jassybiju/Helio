Helio Knowledge Base

========================
Platform Overview
========================

Helio is a healthcare platform that connects patients with licensed doctors for online and offline consultations.

Patients can use Helio to:
- Find doctors
- Book consultations
- Attend online consultations
- Schedule offline clinic visits
- Access consultation reports
- Manage medical history
- Upload medical documents
- Communicate with doctors after consultations
- View and provide doctor reviews

Helio helps patients maintain a continuous healthcare record by organizing consultation information, reports, and medical documents in one place.


========================
Consultation Types
========================

Helio supports two types of consultations:

1. Online Consultation

Patients can consult doctors remotely using available online consultation methods.

Online consultations may include:
- Video consultation
- Voice consultation
- Chat consultation

Available consultation modes depend on the selected doctor and their supported services.


2. Offline Consultation

Patients can book appointments to visit doctors at their clinic or healthcare facility.

Offline consultations allow patients to meet doctors physically at the scheduled location and time.


========================
Doctor Information
========================

Patients can search and choose doctors based on available information such as:

- Medical specialty
- Doctor profile
- Consultation type
- Experience
- Ratings and reviews
- Availability

Patients can view doctor reviews from the appointment booking page before selecting a doctor.

Doctor profiles and availability are provided through Helio's doctor system.


========================
Appointment Booking
========================

Patients can book consultations by selecting a doctor and choosing an available consultation option.

During booking, patients may provide information such as:

- Patient name
- Contact information
- Preferred consultation type
- Reason for consultation
- Preferred appointment time

The available appointment options depend on doctor availability and consultation type.


========================
Consultation Process
========================

During a consultation, patients can discuss their health concerns with the doctor.

After the consultation:

- The doctor provides a consultation report.
- The patient can view the consultation report through Helio.
- The consultation information becomes part of the patient's healthcare history.

Patients can use previous consultation information for future healthcare discussions.


========================
Post Consultation Doctor Chat
========================

After a consultation, patients may continue chatting with the doctor for a limited duration.

The chat availability period is determined by the doctor.

Patients can use this chat period for follow-up communication related to the consultation.

After the doctor-defined chat duration ends, further communication may require another consultation depending on doctor availability and platform options.


========================
Patient Medical History
========================

Helio helps maintain patient healthcare history.

Doctors can access relevant patient history, including:

- Previous consultation reports
- Uploaded lab reports
- Medical documents provided by the patient

Access to patient information is available to authorized healthcare providers involved in patient care.


========================
Lab Reports and Medical Documents
========================

Patients can upload medical documents and lab reports through Helio.

If a doctor requires a lab report:

1. The doctor requests the required report.
2. The patient uploads the requested document.
3. The uploaded report becomes available for the doctor to review.
4. The doctor can refer to the report during future consultations.

Patients should upload accurate and relevant medical documents for doctor review.


========================
Doctor Reviews and Ratings
========================

Patients can view doctor reviews before booking an appointment.

Reviews help patients understand other users' experiences with doctors.

After completing a consultation, patients can submit their own review of the doctor.

A patient review can include feedback about their consultation experience.


========================
Consultation Reports
========================

After completing a consultation, doctors provide consultation reports when applicable.

Consultation reports may contain information related to:

- Consultation details
- Doctor observations
- Recommendations provided by the doctor
- Follow-up information

Patients can access their consultation reports through their Helio account.


========================
Privacy and Security
========================

Helio protects patient information and healthcare records.

Patient information, including:
- Consultation reports
- Lab reports
- Medical documents
- Healthcare history

is accessible only to authorized users.

Helio follows applicable healthcare privacy and security practices.


========================
Frequently Asked Questions
========================

Question:
Does Helio support online consultations?

Answer:
Yes. Helio supports online consultations with doctors through available digital consultation methods.


Question:
Does Helio support offline consultations?

Answer:
Yes. Patients can book offline appointments with doctors when available.


Question:
Will I receive a report after my consultation?

Answer:
Yes. After consultation, doctors can provide a consultation report that patients can access through Helio.


Question:
Can I chat with my doctor after consultation?

Answer:
Yes. Patients may chat with the doctor after consultation during the duration set by the doctor.


Question:
Can doctors see my previous medical history?

Answer:
Yes. Authorized doctors can view relevant patient history, including previous consultation reports and uploaded medical reports.


Question:
How can I share my lab reports with my doctor?

Answer:
If a doctor requests a lab report, the patient can upload the required document through Helio. The doctor can then review the uploaded report.


Question:
Can doctors see uploaded lab reports in future consultations?

Answer:
Yes. Uploaded lab reports can become part of the patient's medical history and may be available for doctors during future consultations.


Question:
Can I see doctor reviews before booking?

Answer:
Yes. Doctor reviews are available on the appointment booking page.


Question:
Can I review my doctor after consultation?

Answer:
Yes. Patients who complete a consultation can submit a review about their doctor.


Question:
Can Helio diagnose medical conditions?

Answer:
Helio provides healthcare platform services. Medical diagnosis and treatment decisions are provided by licensed healthcare professionals.
