Helio Knowledge Base

========================
Platform Overview
========================

Helio is a healthcare platform that connects patients with licensed doctors for online and offline consultations.

Helio helps patients:

- Find doctors
- View doctor profiles
- Book consultations
- Attend online consultations
- Schedule offline clinic visits
- Access consultation reports
- Manage medical history
- Upload medical documents
- Communicate with doctors after consultations
- View doctor reviews


========================
Doctor Discovery
========================

Patients can find doctors on Helio based on available information such as:

- Medical specialty
- Doctor profile
- Experience
- Ratings and reviews
- Consultation availability
- Consultation type

Doctor information, availability, and appointment slots are provided by Helio's doctor system.


========================
Consultation Types
========================

Helio supports:

1. Online Consultation

Patients can consult doctors remotely using supported online methods.

Online consultation methods may include:

- Video consultation
- Voice consultation
- Chat consultation

The available online consultation options depend on the selected doctor.


2. Offline Consultation

Patients can visit doctors physically at their clinic or healthcare facility.

Offline appointments depend on doctor availability and clinic schedules.


========================
Doctor Profiles and Reviews
========================

Patients can view doctor information before selecting a doctor.

Doctor profiles may include:

- Name
- Specialty
- Experience
- Consultation fees
- Reviews and ratings

Patients can view previous patient reviews before booking.

After completing a consultation, patients may provide feedback and ratings about their consultation experience.


========================
Healthcare Records
========================

Helio helps patients maintain their healthcare history.

Healthcare records may include:

- Consultation reports
- Medical documents
- Uploaded lab reports
- Previous consultation information

Authorized healthcare providers can access relevant patient information required for patient care.


========================
Consultation Reports
========================

After a consultation, doctors may provide consultation reports.

Reports may contain:

- Consultation information
- Doctor observations
- Recommendations
- Follow-up details

Patients can access available reports through their Helio account.


========================
Medical Documents and Lab Reports
========================

Patients can upload medical documents and lab reports through Helio.

Uploaded documents can be reviewed by authorized doctors involved in patient care.

Patients should upload accurate and relevant medical information.


========================
Post Consultation Communication
========================

Patients may communicate with doctors after a consultation.

The availability duration of post-consultation communication depends on doctor settings and platform rules.


========================
Privacy and Security
========================

Helio protects patient healthcare information.

Patient information such as:

- Medical history
- Consultation reports
- Lab reports
- Uploaded documents

is accessible only to authorized users.

Helio follows applicable healthcare privacy and security practices.


========================
Frequently Asked Questions
========================

Question:
Does Helio support online consultations?

Answer:
Yes. Helio supports online consultations with doctors through supported digital consultation methods.


Question:
Does Helio support offline consultations?

Answer:
Yes. Patients can schedule offline clinic visits with doctors when available.


Question:
Can I see doctor reviews before selecting a doctor?

Answer:
Yes. Doctor reviews are available before selecting a doctor.


Question:
Can I upload my medical reports?

Answer:
Yes. Patients can upload medical documents and lab reports through Helio.


Question:
Can doctors access my previous medical records?

Answer:
Authorized doctors can access relevant patient healthcare information required for consultation.


Question:
Can Helio diagnose diseases?

Answer:
No. Helio is a healthcare platform that connects patients with licensed doctors. Diagnosis and treatment decisions are made by healthcare professionals.
